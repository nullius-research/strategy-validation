# Requests: strategies sent in for testing

Viewers send strategies in the comments (YouTube, Instagram, Substack). Every request gets a reply that says whether it can be tested. Testable ones go on this list.

To test a strategy I need four things:

1. When you get in, as a rule, not a feeling
2. When you get out: a stop, a target or a signal
3. The market and the timeframe
4. What it claims: a number (win rate, return, pass rate) and a period

Where you saw it (a link) helps, but stays private. Makers, channels and products are never named here: each strategy is listed by its rule. Only markets I have data for can be tested; if the data is missing, the reply says so.

The order on this list says how often a strategy was asked for, not how it performed. A place on the list is not a promise of a date.
